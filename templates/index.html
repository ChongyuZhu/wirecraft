Simulation

Reaching the socket is only part of insertion.

Privileged state policies solve the insertion task. Image-based policies lose success between reaching the socket and seating the connector, where alignment and contact matter.

@@INSERTION_FIGURE@@

The gap extends to routing and seating.

Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT) and a Diffusion Transformer vision-language-action policy (DiT VLA) remain below State Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) on these simulated tasks.
